# Testing Not For Training

Thank you for trying Not For Training before it goes public.

It tells AI companies not to train on your work. It makes your images harder to take. It doesn't hide your site from Google.

This is a test version. You can turn it off at any time, and your site goes back to how it was.

## Install it

You'll get a file called `not-for-training-0.1.0-beta.1.zip`. Don't open it. WordPress needs the zip file as it is.

1. Log in to your WordPress site.
2. Go to **Plugins**, then **Add New Plugin**.
3. Click **Upload Plugin** at the top.
4. Click **Choose File** and pick the zip file.
5. Click **Install Now**.
6. Click **Activate Plugin**.

A message appears at the top of the Plugins page. It says the plugin is on.

To turn it off later, go to **Plugins** and click **Deactivate** under Not For Training.

## What to look at

Go to **Settings**, then **Not For Training**.

**The settings.** The recommended choices are already picked. You don't need to change anything. Read through the page and tell me if anything is confusing.

**The "Is it working?" box.** It's at the top of the page. It checks your site and shows a short list. A green tick means that part works. Anything else comes with a short note on what to do.

## Three things to try

**1. Upload a photo from your phone.**
Use a photo taken with your phone camera. Add it in **Media**, then **Add New Media File**. It should upload and look normal. The plugin removes the location your phone saved in the photo. Tell me if the upload failed, the photo looked wrong, or it took much longer than usual.

**2. Right-click an image on your site while logged out.**
Open your site in a private window, so you're not logged in. Right-click one of your images. The usual "Save image" menu shouldn't appear. Try dragging the image to your desktop too. That shouldn't work either. On a phone, a long press on an image shouldn't offer to save it.

Then try the same thing while logged in. It should work as normal for you.

**3. Check the "Is it working?" box after a day.**
Come back the next day and look at the box again. Click **Check again**. Some hosts and caching plugins only show a problem after a while.
